Lead story · Europe · NATO on alert

MISSILES FALL ON POLAND

Warsaw calls it a deliberate provocation as Russian weapons cross into NATO airspace during the war’s biggest night attack on Ukraine.

Two Russian cruise missiles and a suspected jet-powered drone crashed in south-eastern Poland on Friday, in the middle of a barrage of more than 400 drones and 110 missiles fired at Ukraine overnight — one of the largest attacks of the war. Eight people were killed in Ukraine. In Poland nobody was hurt and nothing was destroyed, but the timing stunned allies: the weapons fell kilometres from Koalicja 26.2, a two-week exercise of eleven nations rehearsing a postwar mission.

Prime Minister Donald Tusk said it “does not look like an accident” and called it “an intentional provocation”. NATO chief Mark Rutte condemned a “grave violation” of Polish airspace; Polish and allied jets, F-16s and Swedish Gripens, were already in the air.

Markets · New York

WALL STREET SLIDES INTO CORRECTION

The S&P 500 and the Dow both entered correction territory on Friday as a sell-off in AI and chip shares dragged US stocks down about 3.7%. Traders dumped the year’s hottest names in a single session, wiping out weeks of gains.

Brazil · Brasília

BOLSONARO RUSHED TO HOSPITAL

Brazil’s imprisoned former president Jair Bolsonaro was taken to hospital with severe abdominal pain, his health declining behind bars. The 71-year-old, serving his sentence after his conviction, was moved for urgent treatment as doctors assessed the cause of the pain.

The hospitalisation lands days after his eldest son Flávio lost an election and moved his family to the United States.

SEVERE PAIN — linked to his long-running abdominal troubles after the 2018 stabbing.
Cyberattack · Finance

BIG BANKS HIT BY HACKERS

JPMorgan Chase, Visa and Mastercard all reported cyberattacks on third-party vendors on Friday. The firms said their core payment networks were unaffected — but the triple disclosure rattled a financial system that runs on outsourced suppliers.

Summit · Shenzhen

TRUMP, XI, PUTIN LEAVE THIN

The first trilateral summit of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders ended in Shenzhen with few concrete results. President Zelenskyy called the attempt to “redraw the world” a failure.

Europe · Brussels

UK AND EU SIGN THREE DEALS

Prime Minister Andy Burnham signed agreements on food trade, carbon markets and youth mobility at a Brussels summit — the deepest UK–EU reset since Brexit.
